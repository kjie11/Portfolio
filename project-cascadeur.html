Cascadeur Motion Capture

A video-based motion capture exercise in Cascadeur, exploring how recorded movement can be translated into editable character animation.

The practice focused on reviewing the captured motion against the source video, then refining poses and timing on the animation timeline.

Practice Focus

  • Using recorded video as the motion reference
  • Reviewing captured movement on a 3D character
  • Refining poses and timing in Cascadeur
