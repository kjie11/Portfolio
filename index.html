Hi, I'm Ruyan Qin.

I am a Unity/XR Developer and Interaction Designer focused on real-time interaction, gameplay systems, and hardware integration across VR, AR, and WebGL.

I design and build end-to-end prototypes in Unity and C#, combining UX research with technical development to turn gesture inputs, sensor data, and player behaviour into intuitive, robust, and testable experiences.
