Pizza Delivery

Pizza Delivery is a casual Unity WebGL driving game developed during my internship at Dream Reality Interactive. I designed and built the tutorial and first level around a clear pickup, delivery, reward, upgrade, and area-unlock loop.

Across three Poki test rounds of approximately 500 players each, I used player behaviour to identify friction in onboarding, progression, and feedback. Average playtime increased from 44 seconds to 1 minute 53 seconds after the three rounds—an increase of approximately 157%.

Gameplay screenshot · Driving, active order, countdown, and reward feedback

The Problem

Early players were leaving before they could understand the full delivery loop. The experience needed to communicate the next action quickly, make short- and mid-term goals visible, and give driving events enough feedback to remain readable in a browser game.

Process

I reviewed player behaviour after each Poki test round, translated the observed friction into design priorities, and iterated the tutorial, task guidance, progression, and moment-to-moment feedback. Git-based versioning and automated WebGL builds kept each test release repeatable.

Key Work

  • Defined the pickup, delivery, reward, and upgrade loop
  • Designed order, countdown, garage, and area-unlock progression
  • Reworked onboarding prompts and task guidance from player behaviour
  • Layered audio, particles, camera, and UI feedback into driving events
  • Set up repeatable WebGL builds for Poki and itch.io testing

Solution

  • A tutorial and first level that introduce driving through immediate delivery goals
  • Standard, timed, and VIP orders with distinct rewards and urgency
  • Vehicle upgrades and area unlocks that connect individual deliveries to longer-term progress
  • Clearer prompts, target guidance, and multimodal feedback for important actions

Results

  • 3 Poki test rounds
  • Approximately 500 players per round
  • 44 seconds to 1 minute 53 seconds average playtime
  • Approximately 157% increase in average playtime

My Role

  • Game Designer: Defined the core loop, order types, progression, and onboarding
  • Unity Prototyper: Extended vehicle control and implemented gameplay, UI, and feedback systems
  • Experience Designer: Turned player behaviour into priorities across three test rounds

Learnings

The project reinforced that early-session clarity is part of the core game system, not a layer added after it. Behavioural data helped prioritize concrete changes, while repeatable browser builds made each iteration easier to test and compare.

Evidence record: DOC-001–003. Round-by-round retention, completion rate, and change-level attribution remain unavailable.
